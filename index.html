US hours of service trip planner with day grids that start today, four clocks, limit warnings, an automatic trip planner and a split sleeper toggle

Duty status grid. Left and right arrows move between segments, 15 minutes on a computer and 30 minutes on a phone or tablet. Up and down arrows change the status of the segment. Keys 1 to 4 set off duty, sleeper berth, driving, or on duty. Hold shift while moving to paint the status across segments. Home and End jump to the start or end of the day. Page up and page down move one hour. Enter sets the clocks to this time. To move the clocks with a pointer, drag the red marker at the top of a grid.
