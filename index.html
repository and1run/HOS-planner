US hours of service trip planner with day grids that start today, four clocks, limit warnings, an automatic trip planner and a split sleeper toggle

Duty status grid. Left and right arrows move between 15 minute segments. Up and down arrows change the status of the segment. Keys 1 to 4 set off duty, sleeper berth, driving, or on duty. Hold shift while moving to paint the status across segments. Home and End jump to the start or end of the day. Page up and page down move one hour. Enter sets the clocks to this time. To move the clocks with a pointer, drag the red marker at the top of a grid.

How to use the grids
Tap or drag on a row to set a 15 minute block. To move the clocks, drag the red marker at the top of a grid or tap the hour ruler. Red means driving over a limit. Today starts rested (10 hours or more off); if you're mid-shift, mark the hours already worked. With a keyboard, use arrow keys to move, 1 to 4 to set the status, and Enter to move the clocks here.
